Word.tips alternative

Word.tips offers a word finder for Scrabble, Words With Friends and Wordle; daily hints and answers for Wordle, Connections, Strands, Spelling Bee, Pips and Contexto; a mini crossword and a word game; and a mobile app. If you want another free option, this is what thewordfinder.io has.

What you get

  • Word finder: up to 15 letters, ? for blank tiles, Scrabble and Words With Friends points, filters for starts with, ends with and contains.
  • Daily hints for Wordle, Connections, Strands and Spelling Bee, with the answer hidden until you reveal it.
  • Free browser games: Wordle Unlimited, Quad and Octo Wordle, Sudoku, Word Search and Connections Unlimited, each with a daily puzzle and an unlimited mode.
  • Anagram solver, word unscrambler and Wordle solver.
  • Open word data: the lists behind it are published here, so any result can be checked.

Which to use

If you use Word.tips for daily hints, the closest match is our hints-first daily pages; if you use it for racks, our word finder works the same way, with ? for blanks.
