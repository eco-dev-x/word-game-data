How to solve Wordle: 7 tips backed by data

Every tip below comes from scoring guesses against 1,574 everyday five-letter answers, not from gut feeling.

1. Open with RAISE

RAISE leaves 37.8 answers on average, the best of all 8,613 allowed guesses. CRANE leaves 48.3; ADIEU leaves 80.8. Full ranking.

2. Don't chase vowels

Vowel-heavy openers feel productive but narrow the answer least. Consonants and their positions do the work: E, A, O are the most common vowels, but one or two vowels per guess is enough.

3. Use a fixed second word

RAISE then DONUT leaves about 4.1 answers whatever RAISE shows. Best second words.

4. Think about position

E ends 295 of the answers and S starts 243. Test letters where they usually sit. Letter frequency by position.

5. Move yellows

A yellow letter is in the word but not there. Put it somewhere new on your next guess, so it can turn green or rule out another spot.

6. Watch for repeated letters

Words like SPEED or SKILL trip people up. If few answers fit, consider a double letter before burning a guess on a new one.

7. Narrow it down, then guess

When several words fit (the _IGHT trap: FIGHT, LIGHT, MIGHT, NIGHT…), play a word that tests several of those first letters at once rather than guessing them one by one. List what's left with the Wordle word finder or solver.
