Wordle solver and helper

Type a guess you played, then tap each tile until it matches your colours (grey, yellow, green). The list shows every answer that still fits, and the best next guesses.

Add a guess to start. Not sure what to open with? RAISE is the best common opener in our ranking.

How it works

Every one of the 1,574 answers is checked against your guesses using the game's colour rules (greens first, then yellows from the letters left). "Best next guess" picks the remaining answer that, on average, leaves the fewest possibilities. Everything runs in your browser; the code is in this page.
