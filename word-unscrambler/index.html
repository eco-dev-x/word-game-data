Word unscrambler

Type up to 15 scrambled letters (use ? for a blank tile) to see every word they make, longest first.

How it works

A word fits if it needs no letter more often than you have it; each shortfall uses up a blank. It checks all 168,434 words in the ENABLE-based list in your browser. The method is explained in how to build an anagram solver.

For Scrabble and Words With Friends points, filters (starts with, ends with) and definitions, use the full word unscrambler on thewordfinder.io.
